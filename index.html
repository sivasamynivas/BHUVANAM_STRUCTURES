    Before we start

    Please share a soil test report. Without one, we design for the worst-case soil bearing capacity.

    All designs follow IS standards only.

    Structural design begins after the architectural plan is final, and after approval by the competent authority.

    Major plan changes after structural drawings are issued may revise charges.

    Trial changes beyond the limit, and extra site visits, are charged by scope and location.

    Package offers apply in and around Coimbatore. Listed prices are flexible and can be adjusted after discussion.
